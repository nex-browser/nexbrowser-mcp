# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, professional place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, browser data, or identifying information.
- Accept technical feedback in good faith and focus on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, deliberate disclosure of private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Security vulnerabilities must follow the process in `SECURITY.md` once published and must not be reported through a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and consistently as practical.
